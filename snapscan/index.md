# SnapScan – Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** November 4, 2026

**Developer:** Waseem Ashraf

SnapScan is a document scanning application that helps you scan, enhance, organize, and export documents as PDF files or images.

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information SnapScan accesses, how it is used, where it is stored, and how you can manage or delete it.

## 1. Introduction

SnapScan is a document scanning application that allows users to scan, enhance, organize, and export documents as PDF files or images.

This Privacy Policy explains how SnapScan handles information when you use the application.

## 2. Information We Collect

SnapScan does not require users to create an account or provide personal registration information.

The application may access the following information and resources when you use its features:

- **Camera:** To scan physical documents.
- **Photos and files:** To import, save, and export documents.
- **App preferences:** Settings such as theme mode and scanning preferences.

Scanned documents and related files are stored locally on your device.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

Information and permissions are used to provide the application's functionality, including:

- Scanning physical documents.
- Importing images.
- Enhancing scanned documents.
- Creating PDF files.
- Saving images to your gallery.
- Printing and sharing documents.
- Remembering application preferences.

## 4. Document Storage

SnapScan stores scanned documents and application-related files locally on your device.

The application does not provide its own account-based cloud synchronization or document-upload service.

If you choose to share, print, or export a document, the selected destination or third-party application may process that document according to its own privacy policy.

## 5. Third-Party Services

SnapScan uses Google ML Kit Document Scanner functionality through Google services.

Google Play services may process technical, diagnostic, or usage information as part of providing their functionality. Such processing is governed by Google's applicable privacy policies.

SnapScan does not include advertising or analytics services in its currently declared Flutter dependencies.

## 6. Data Sharing

The developer does not operate a backend service to collect or store your scanned documents.

Documents are shared with other applications or services only when you initiate an action such as sharing, printing, or exporting.

## 7. Data Security

SnapScan uses local device storage for its document management features.

Users should protect their devices with appropriate security measures. The developer cannot guarantee the security of files after they are exported or shared outside the application.

## 8. Data Deletion

You can delete documents stored within SnapScan using the application's available document management features.

Files previously exported to your gallery, file manager, or another application may need to be deleted separately.

Uninstalling the application may remove its app-specific local data, subject to Android storage and backup behavior.

## 9. Children's Privacy

SnapScan is a general-purpose document scanning utility and is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

## 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when application features, permissions, or data-handling practices change.

Any updated version will be published at this same URL.
